About the data

Where it comes from. Temperature sensors on commercial fishing gear, fishing-vessel research fleets, state monitoring and NOAA/IOOS surveys:

  • eMOLT and Maine Coast Fishermen's Association: bottom readings while gear is fishing, and profiles while it is set and hauled.
  • Commercial Fisheries Research Foundation (OnDeck ERDDAP): fixed-gear bottom sensors, trap-haul casts, Shelf Research Fleet CTD casts (with WHOI), South Fork Wind survey CTD casts and receiver thermistors, and demersal glider bottom data.
  • Mass. DMF Cape Cod Bay dissolved oxygen monitoring: bottom temperature from loggers on study-fleet lobster traps, plus DMF's long-running fixed bottom temperature stations.
  • NOAA NEFSC ECOMON and survey CTD casts: full water-column profiles.
  • IOOS Glider DAC: profiles from ocean gliders in the region (delayed-mode copies when available).

FIShBOT grid. Readings are averaged onto NOAA's FIShBOT grid, the same 7 km (3.8 nm) squares FIShBOT uses, by calendar month. Bottom squares and profiles both cover the last 3 years; profiles are in 5 m layers. Tapping a square lists which sources fed it.

Model layer. The DOPPIO model switch fills squares that have no observations with the DOPPIO ocean model (Rutgers, 7 km, data assimilating), shown hatched, and adds them to the month bars as hatched segments. Monthly means come from daily model averages sampled weekly for bottom temperature (last 3 years) and every 10 days for profiles (top 150 m), both over the last 3 years. Against the observations here, model bottom temperatures differ by about 1 °C on average.

Target depth. For midwater species the app interpolates each square's profile, finds where it is in the species' band, and reports the middle of the thickest in-band layer.

Updated daily. The files behind the map are rebuilt from these servers once a day. The dot at the top shows when.

Depth contours are GEBCO 2024 bathymetry, in fathoms.

How to use
  • Pick a species and a preset area, or tap Rectangle or Polygon and draw your own on the map.
  • For a polygon, tap each corner, then tap Done or the first corner again.
  • On the results, tap a month in the strip to change months. Zoom in to see each square's temperature and depth.
  • Use Change to go back and pick another species or area.
  • The address bar link opens straight to the same view, so you can send it to someone.
  • For midwater species, each green square's number is the target depth in fathoms. Tap it for the full in-band layer.
  • Turn on the DOPPIO model switch on the results screen to fill unsampled squares and the month bars with the ocean model (hatched).
